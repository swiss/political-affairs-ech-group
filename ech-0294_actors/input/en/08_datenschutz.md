# Data protection

     Translation of the German chapter 08_datenschutz.md. -->

This standard describes data about natural persons. Such data are personal data within the meaning of Art. 5 lit. a of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Information on party affiliation, mandates and interest links allows political opinions to be inferred and therefore constitutes sensitive personal data (Art. 5 lit. c FADP). For cantonal and municipal bodies, the respective cantonal data protection and freedom of information acts apply.

As politicians or members of public bodies, the persons described are of public interest. Much of their information is already published today on the basis of disclosure obligations or the principle of transparency. The standard does not, however, create a legal basis for publication, nor does it impose an obligation to publish individual attributes.

Whether and to what extent data are published is the responsibility of the publishing body. It decides on the basis of the legal provisions applicable to it and takes the necessary precautions, for example by flagging information not intended for publication or making it visible only internally.

The following points require particular attention:

- **Data minimisation.** Optional elements, in particular date of birth, address and contact details, are populated only where they are necessary for the purpose of the publication.
- **Private residential addresses.** Their publication is the subject of motions [24.3425](https://www.parlament.ch/en/ratsbetrieb/suche-curia-vista/geschaeft?AffairId=20243425) (replacing the private residential address as identifier of the authors of popular initiatives) and [23.3913](https://www.parlament.ch/en/ratsbetrieb/suche-curia-vista/geschaeft?AffairId=20233913) (preventing the obligation to publish the residential addresses of members of parliament). Where an address is required, a business or correspondence address is to be used where possible.
- **Aggregation.** Combining several sources can produce personality profiles that go beyond the purpose of any individual publication. Whoever combines data according to this standard re-examines the purpose limitation.
- **Historical values in references.** `PersonReference` and `GroupReference` retain local attributes as of the time of linking. They must not be interpreted as the current state.
