# Introduction

## Context: public council operations

At federal, cantonal and communal level, councils and assemblies convene, deliberate on political affairs, take decisions and scrutinise the executive.

## Relationship to other eCH standards

eCH-0293 is part of an ecosystem of standards for political data:

### eCH-0294: Political Actors
Defines persons, bodies and groups in the political context. eCH-0293 references these actors via `actor_id` (e.g. which parliament, which person voted).

### eCH-0295: Parliamentary Affairs
Describes the life cycle of political affairs. AgendaItems in eCH-0293 point to the corresponding affairs via `affair_id`.

### eCH-0296: Laws and Legal Texts
Records the results of the parliamentary process — the laws and enactments adopted.

### eCH-0297: Public Consultations
Structures consultation procedures, which are often the starting point for parliamentary affairs.
